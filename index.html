stickopedia uploader

A personal script that publishes my own videos to my own YouTube channels.

What it does

I make pixel-art science explainer videos. They are rendered on my computer, and each one has a written title, description, tag list and thumbnail prepared alongside it. This tool reads those files and uploads the finished video to the YouTube channel it belongs to, scheduled for a publication date I choose.

It exists to remove the copy-and-paste step between the folder where a video is produced and the YouTube upload form. That is its entire purpose.

Who uses it

Only me. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and it has no users other than the owner of the channels it publishes to. It runs from a command line on a single computer.

What access it asks for

It requests the YouTube scopes needed to upload a video and set its thumbnail (youtube.upload and youtube) on channels I own. It does not read, modify or access anyone else's YouTube data.

Privacy

It collects nothing. The full statement is here: Privacy Policy.
